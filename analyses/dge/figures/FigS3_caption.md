**Figure S3. Differential expression of mitophagy genes in *Myotis myotis* fibroblasts mapped onto the KEGG Mitophagy – animal pathway (hsa04137).** Boxes are coloured by log₂ fold change in *M. myotis* relative to house mouse (*Mus musculus*) fibroblasts for differentially expressed genes (|log₂FC| ≥ 2 and adjusted p < 0.05; DESeq2): purple, downregulated; green to yellow, upregulated; values beyond ±7 are shown at the end colours. Grey boxes are genes measured but not differentially expressed; white boxes are genes not measured. Where KEGG groups paralogs in a single box, the box is coloured and labelled by the differentially expressed member. Upon mitochondrial damage, PINK1 accumulates on the outer mitochondrial membrane, where it recruits and phosphorylates PRKN (Parkin). FBXO7 (not pictured) is proposed to stabilize PINK1 and promote mitophagy priming. Activated PRKN ubiquitinates outer mitochondrial membrane substrates, enabling recruitment of selective autophagy receptors (OPTN, NDP52/CALCOCO2) and LC3 (MAP1LC3A)-positive phagophores, leading to mitochondrial sequestration and degradation. Genes under intensified selection in hyphy RELAX (FBXO7, OPA1) are indicated by k > 1, and genes under relaxed selection (HUWE1) by k < 1; tagged boxes have a heavy outline.
